# About Me

I am a **Machine Learning Engineer** with a multidisciplinary background spanning **Electrical Engineering, Biomedical Engineering, and Computer Science**.

My journey started with a fascination for mathematics and signal processing, which led me to pursue Electrical Engineering and later Biomedical Engineering. Long before machine learning became mainstream, I was studying how signals can reveal hidden information about complex systems — from electronic circuits to the human brain.

While my formal education provided strong foundations in mathematics, modelling, and signal processing, I taught myself software engineering, machine learning, artificial intelligence, and modern data science practices through years of hands-on research and industry work.

Today, I focus on designing scalable AI systems that transform large-scale biomedical data into clinically meaningful insights.

# Career Journey

### Brain-Computer Interfaces & Neurotechnology

My early research focused on:

- Brain-Computer Interfaces (BCI)
- EEG analysis
- Digital Signal Processing (DSP)
- Neural data modelling
- Understanding cognitive and mental health states from brain signals

This work led to projects involving biomarker discovery and mental health diagnosis using advanced signal processing and machine learning techniques.

### Biomedical AI & Population Health

Over the last several years, my focus has shifted toward large-scale biomedical data analysis and real-world health applications.

I have worked extensively with:

- UK Biobank
- All of Us Research Program
- Our Future Health
- Generation Scotland

These resources provide access to some of the world's largest collections of health, genomic, behavioural, and electronic health record data.

### Machine Learning Engineering

Currently, I work as a **Machine Learning Engineer at Hurdle**, where I design scalable systems for biomedical data processing, biomarker discovery, and AI-driven health research.

A significant portion of my recent work involves:

- Processing large-scale biomedical datasets
- Designing reproducible research workflows
- Developing scalable biomarker discovery frameworks
- Building production-grade data pipelines
- Translating research ideas into deployable systems
