# About Me

I am a **Machine Learning Engineer** with a multidisciplinary background spanning **Electrical Engineering, Biomedical Engineering, and Computer Science**.

My journey started with a fascination for mathematics and signal processing, which led me to Electrical Engineering and later Biomedical Engineering. Long before machine learning became mainstream, I was studying how signals can reveal hidden information about complex systems, from electronic circuits to the human brain.

My formal education gave me strong foundations in mathematics, modelling, and signal processing. I then taught myself software engineering, machine learning, AI, and modern data science through years of hands-on research and industry work.

Today, I design scalable AI systems that turn large-scale biomedical data into clinically meaningful insights.

# Career Journey

### 🧠 Brain-Computer Interfaces & Neurotechnology

My early research focused on:

- Brain-Computer Interfaces (BCI)
- EEG analysis
- Digital Signal Processing (DSP)
- Neural data modelling
- Explainable deep learning for time-series data
- Understanding cognitive and mental health states from brain signals

This work led to projects in biomarker discovery and mental health diagnosis using advanced signal processing and machine learning, including [**EEG-ITNet**](https://arxiv.org/abs/2204.06947), an explainable architecture for EEG time-series classification.

### 🏥 Biomedical AI & Population Health

Over the last several years, my focus has shifted toward large-scale biomedical data analysis and real-world health applications, in both industry and academic partnerships.

I have worked extensively with:

- UK Biobank
- All of Us Research Program
- Our Future Health
- Generation Scotland

These resources hold some of the world's largest collections of health, genomic, behavioural, and electronic health record data.

### ⚙️ Machine Learning Engineering

Currently, I work as a **Machine Learning Engineer at Hurdle**, where I build scalable systems for biomedical data processing, biomarker discovery, and AI-driven health research.

A big part of my recent work involves:

- Harmonising health data across cohorts, coding systems, and vocabularies
- Discovering and validating biomarkers for disease risk prediction
- Building transformer-based foundation models for longitudinal health trajectories
- Designing reproducible research workflows
- Building production-grade, containerised data and ML pipelines
- Turning research ideas into deployable systems

### 🎓 Teaching & Mentoring

I have also taught programming, databases, AI, and networks at university-preparation level, and I still enjoy making complex ideas simple.
